A book sharing station built to live outside.

StoryStop is a book sharing station for parks, schools, and neighborhoods. It's molded as a single piece of thick plastic, so it holds up to weather and heavy use better than a wooden box on a post.

How it works

1

Take a book

Anyone walking by can open it and pick out something to read. There's no library card and no due date.

2

Leave a book

When you finish a book at home, bring it back or leave a different one in its place.

3

Pass it on

The people who use the station are the ones who keep it stocked.

Why mold it out of plastic?

  • One solid piece

    Rotational molding makes a hollow shell with no seams or joints, so there's nothing to pull apart or leak.

  • Color all the way through

    The color is in the plastic itself, which means there's no paint to chip, peel, or redo every spring.

  • Built for outside

    Kayaks and playground slides are made the same way, and they stay outdoors for years.

  • Rounded everywhere

    Molded parts have soft corners and no sharp edges, which matters at a child's height.
