VertiGarden is an aeroponic tower for growing greens, herbs and strawberries at home without soil. Plants sit in pockets along the sides of the tower, and their roots are misted with water and nutrients from the inside.

Grow more

The plants stack vertically instead of spreading across a garden bed, so a balcony or a corner of the kitchen is enough space.

Use less

Water the roots don't absorb drains back to the base and is pumped through again, so very little goes to waste.

Eat better

Produce is at its best right after it's picked, and the tower keeps it a few steps from the kitchen.

How aeroponics works

  1. Seedlings sit in the pockets with their roots hanging down inside the hollow tower.

  2. A small pump in the base pushes water mixed with nutrients up to the top.

  3. The water rains down over the bare roots as a mist, so they get air and food at the same time.

  4. Whatever is left collects in the base and goes around again on the next cycle.
