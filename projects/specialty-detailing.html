How a car goes through the shop

STEP 1

Wash and decontaminate

Hand wash, iron removal and clay to get everything off the surface.

STEP 2

Correct the paint

Machine polishing to take out swirls, scratches and oxidation.

STEP 3

Protect

Coating, film or tint goes on by hand in a controlled space.

STEP 4

Final inspection

Every panel gets checked under lights before the keys go back.

Owner operated

Steve started by helping his dad clean the family car and went on to detail his own truck. He still works on every vehicle that comes through the shop, using SunTek film and Dr. Beasley's coatings.
