DR. BEASLEY'S CERAMIC COATING

Ceramic coating

A hard, glossy layer that repels water, resists UV and chemical stains, and makes the car far easier to keep clean. Used cars can be coated too, as long as the paint is corrected first.

Warranties are backed by the manufacturer and cover fading, oxidation, and loss of water beading under normal use. To keep a warranty valid, the car needs a yearly maintenance wash at the shop and the aftercare steps below. Wheels, glass, trim, and interior coating are available as add ons, and extra correction steps are quoted separately.

AFTERCARE

Keep it looking like day one

Ceramic coating
  • Wait 7 days before the first wash.
  • Hand wash only, with no automatic car washes.
  • Use pH neutral soap and a soft wash mitt.
  • Book the yearly maintenance wash to keep the warranty valid.
Paint protection film
  • Don't wash it for the first 7 days, and keep it out of the rain if you can.
  • Leave the film edges alone while it settles.
  • Use pH neutral soap and skip petroleum based waxes.
  • Keep a pressure washer at least 12 inches from the edges.
  • A few small bubbles early on are normal.
Window tint
  • Keep the windows up for 3 to 5 days.
  • Wait 5 days before cleaning the inside of the glass.
  • Use an ammonia free cleaner and a microfiber towel, never a razor or scraper.
  • Some haze is normal and clears within two weeks, faster if you park in the sun.
