CUYAHOGA VALLEY NATIONAL PARK, SINCE 2007

Wine, wood fired pizza, and local art inside the national park.

Sarah's Vineyard grows nine grape varieties on its hill in Cuyahoga Valley National Park, right across from Blossom Music Center. Pour a glass in the tasting room, find a seat on the deck, or warm up by the pavilion fireplace.

    FIND A SEAT

    Five places to settle in

    You get table service in the tasting room, on the deck, and in the butterfly garden. The pavilion and its picnic tables run on bar service, and the full menu and wine list are available everywhere.

    Tasting room

    THE HEART OF THE WINERY

    The bar, the kitchen, and the main seating, all inside a barn frame that was built before the Civil War. The walls double as the art gallery.

    The deck

    PARTIES UP TO 6

    Looks right out over the grapevines and has a small fire pit for cooler nights.

    Butterfly garden

    AT THE ENTRANCE

    Exotic plants, a koi pond, and a small waterfall, with picnic tables for bigger groups.

    The pavilion

    PARTIES UP TO 12, SEASONAL

    A three story wood burning fireplace built from stones off Cleveland's East 14th Street, under white oak trusses that span more than 36 feet. Browns games play here in the fall, along with Guardians and Cavs games during open hours. Open weekends as weather permits.

    The Loft

    PRIVATE EVENTS, UP TO 40

    The winery's first addition, finished in 2011. It sits on two levels overlooking the tasting room and is booked for private parties.

    OUR STORY

    Named for Sarah

    Margaret and Mike Lytz opened Sarah's Vineyard in 2007 on fields that had been left to go wild. The vineyard is named for Margaret's daughter, Sarah, and the fields are cared for in her memory.

    Mike grows the grapes and makes the wine, and Margaret runs the art side of the winery. Staff and volunteers helped bring the place to life, turning a forgotten stretch of the valley back into countryside.

    1. Before the Civil WarThe hand hewn timber frame that became the winery is raised near what's now Cloverleaf High School in Medina County.
    2. 2007The frame is taken down, moved into the Cuyahoga Valley, finished in cherry, aspen, poplar, walnut, and maple, and opened as the winery.
    3. 2011The Loft is finished as the first addition.
    4. TodayNine grape varieties grow on the hill: Vidal, Seyval, Frontenac, Cabernet Franc, Chambourcin, Traminette, Cayuga, Niagara, and Rubiana.
